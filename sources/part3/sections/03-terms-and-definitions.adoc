== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the terms and definitions given in
<<onnx-part-1>> apply, together with the following.

[[vector-outcome]]
=== outcome

result of applying one <<test-vector-t>> to an implementation, being one of
_pass_, _fail_, _unsupported_ or _error_

[[test-vector-t]]
=== test vector

model, together with input values, expected output values and the tolerance
against which they are compared
