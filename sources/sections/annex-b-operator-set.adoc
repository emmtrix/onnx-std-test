[appendix,obligation=informative]
== Known gaps and divergences

This annex records, for the working group's use, the points at which the
present ONNX specification and its reference implementation do not yet supply
what a normative standard requires. It is informative and is expected to shrink
to nothing before this document is submitted anywhere.

[cols="1,3,1"]
.Known gaps between current ONNX practice and this document
|===
| Clause | Gap | Severity

| <<subclause-tolerances>>
| No specified numerical tolerance; the test suite's tolerances are a harness
  convention and are not defined for the reduced-precision element types.
| Blocking

| Clause 2, <<serialization>>
| Protocol Buffers has no citable specification with a stable identifier.
| Blocking

| <<subclause-strings>>
| `STRING` elements are octet strings in the implementation and UTF-8 by
  assumption in much tooling.
| Major

| Clause 5 (type inference)
| Type and shape inference is relied upon normatively but specified only by
  implementation.
| Major

| Clause 6 (initializers)
| Two readings of the initializer/graph-input relationship are in circulation.
| Major

| Clause 7 (determinism)
| No statement of which operators may be non-deterministic.
| Major

| Clause 10 (compatibility)
| Backward compatibility is stated as an obligation here; runtimes do not
  universally meet it.
| Major

| Clause 8 (operator specifications)
| Not all operators supply shape inference and error conditions in a form that
  can be restated normatively.
| Major

| <<serialization>> (canonical form)
| No canonical serialization, blocking model signing and reproducible hashes.
| Minor
|===
