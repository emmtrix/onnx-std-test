[[maintenance]]
== Maintenance and extension

=== Custom domains

A model MAY use operators in a <<domain>> not defined by <<onnx-part-2>>.

A consumer that does not implement such a domain SHALL report the operator as
unsupported under <<conformance>>, and SHALL NOT evaluate the model.

=== Experimental operators

This document does not recognize an experimental status for an operator. Every
operator of an operator set published under <<onnx-part-2>> is subject without
exception to <<operator-set-evolution>>.

An operator whose definition is not yet settled SHALL be published in a
<<domain>> of its own, and is a custom operator under the preceding subclause.

NOTE: Upstream reached the same position. The serialized form of an operator
set carries a status of `EXPERIMENTAL` or `STABLE`, and an operator marked
`STABLE` may not change; but experimental operators have not been accepted into
the ONNX domains since release 1.5, and the operators that had been are
withdrawn. The field remains in the format, and this document gives it no
effect.

A producer SHOULD replace a withdrawn experimental operator with the operators
that supersede it. <<onnx-part-2>> records the correspondence.

=== Adding an operator

The procedure by which an operator enters an <<operator-set>> is specified in
<<onnx-part-2>>. The constraints that procedure must respect are in
<<versioning>>.
