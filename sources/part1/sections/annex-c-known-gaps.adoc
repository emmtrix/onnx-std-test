[appendix,obligation=informative]
== Known gaps and divergences

This annex records, for the working group's use, the points at which the
present ONNX specification and its reference implementation do not yet supply
what a normative standard requires. It is informative and is expected to shrink
to nothing before this document is submitted anywhere.

Every clause carrying an editorial note has at least one row here. A row may
cover several related notes in the same clause; a clause with a note and no row
is an omission.

[cols="1,3,1"]
.Known gaps between current ONNX practice and this document
|===
| Clause | Gap | Severity

| <<tolerances>>
| No specified numerical tolerance; the test suite's tolerances are a harness
  convention and are not defined for the reduced-precision element types.
  <<onnx-part-3>> cannot pass or fail a vector without them.
| Blocking

| Clause 2, <<serialization>>
| Protocol Buffers has no citable specification with a stable identifier.
| Blocking

| <<validation-clause>>
| The checks a validating consumer owes are drafted but not yet stated as
  testable conditions with severities.
| Blocking

| <<security>>
| Threat model, path resolution rules and resource bounds are unwritten.
| Major

| <<string-elements>>
| `STRING` elements are octet strings in the implementation and UTF-8 by
  assumption in much tooling.
| Major

| Clause 6 (reduced precision)
| The sub-8-bit float and integer types and their packing are unspecified here.
| Major

| <<inference>>
| Type and shape inference is relied upon normatively but specified only by
  implementation.
| Major

| Clause 7 (initializers)
| Two readings of the initializer/graph-input relationship are in circulation.
| Major

| <<graph-semantics>> (determinism)
| No statement of which operators may be non-deterministic.
| Major

| <<graph-semantics>> (control flow, functions)
| The semantics of `If`, `Loop` and `Scan`, and the normative status of
  function bodies, are unwritten.
| Major

| <<versioning>> (compatibility)
| Backward compatibility is stated as an obligation here; runtimes do not
  universally meet it.
| Major

| <<numerical>> (special values, reassociation)
| NaN and infinity behaviour, and the permitted reassociation, are unwritten;
  the tolerance cannot be derived until they are.
| Major

| <<external-data>>
| Reference resolution and integrity are unspecified.
| Major

| Clause 4 (profiles)
| No conformance profile is defined, so the class system has no granularity.
| Minor

| <<canonical-form>>
| No canonical serialization, blocking model signing and reproducible hashes.
| Minor

| <<maintenance>>
| The status of experimental operators, and the amendment process, are
  unwritten.
| Minor

| Annex B
| The Protocol Buffers schema is not yet restated normatively as a table of
  fields with obligations.
| Blocking

| <<information-model>>, <<graph-structure>>
| Subgraph scoping — which names a subgraph may capture, and whether shadowing
  is permitted — is unspecified structurally, independently of the control-flow
  semantics above.
| Major

| Clause 6 (type equality)
| When two types are equal, and when a value of one type may be supplied where
  another is expected, is unstated; this bounds what <<inference>> must prove.
| Major

| Clause 12 (encoding)
| The admissible subset of the Protocol Buffers wire format — unknown fields,
  repeated field ordering, maximum message size — is unstated.
| Major

| Clause 4 (verbal forms)
| The document uses RFC 2119 capitalised keywords; ISO/IEC Directives Part 2
  requires "shall/should/may/can". An editorial migration, not a technical one.
| Minor
|===
